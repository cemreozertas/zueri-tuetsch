For German speakers in Zürich

You already speak most of it.
You just can't hear it yet.

Zürichdeutsch isn't a new language. It's Standard German run through a dozen predictable sound shifts, plus a few hundred everyday words. Learn the shifts, memorise the words, and practise reversing what you hear.

Ich ha hüt kei Ziit. → Ich habe heute keine Zeit.
